# VentureStress AI

A business idea stress tester that turns uncertain assumptions into practical validation experiments.

## The Unique Kernel

Challenge a business idea across four domains: Customer Demand, Competition & Differentiation, Pricing & Economics, and Operations & Delivery. Identify two or three critical assumptions in each domain (8–12 total) and explain how to test them. Prioritize using impact and uncertainty with a clear rationale, without inventing a numerical viability score. Risks appear within the relevant domains.

## Who It's For

Early-stage entrepreneurs deciding what to validate before investing in a business idea. The learner selected entrepreneurs as the initial audience on September 29, 2026.

## The Core Loop

Describe an idea, run an analysis, inspect critical assumptions, and choose a practical validation experiment. Users can return with a revised idea after learning from actual tests; experiment tracking is outside this proof of concept.

## Inspiration & Identity

An analytical, trustworthy interface with structured sections and constructive challenges. The learner requested professional styling and visuals for entrepreneurs, delegating the detailed design. The implementation direction is a light editorial workspace with navy typography, teal accents, readable cards, and informative priority visuals.

## Why This Matters to the Learner

The learner wants to build and deploy this project for Build With AI: Basics and learn advanced coding through the work.

## What "Working" Looks Like

A user submits a business idea and receives real AI-generated, validated structured analysis, critical assumptions, and actionable validation experiments. The memorable result is knowing which assumption to test first and how. Evidence, assumptions and uncertainties must be distinguished.

## The POC Boundary

One idea-input journey and one server-side analysis request. Results include an executive summary, customer problem, competition, economics, operations, risks, assumptions and validation actions. Include loading, invalid-input, failure and retry states, and a responsive results interface. No server-side report persistence.

The learner has requested source publication to https://github.com/mohdhuzkhn/dev-post-hackathon and deployment. Gemini's free API tier and Netlify Free were explicitly accepted on September 29, 2026, including the discussed free-tier data-policy tradeoff.

The learner also requests conducting real business experiments. Execution will be a separate manual pilot, as confirmed by the learner. The pilot subject is an AI tutor clone platform where online teachers create AI clones that conduct classes independently. Participant access remains open and does not block the app build. Do not imply any real-world validation has occurred merely because an experiment plan was generated.

## Later

Evidence-backed research and citations, saved analyses, idea comparison, and experiment tracking.

## Explicitly Cut

- Accounts, payments and collaboration: unnecessary to prove the core journey.
- Database: reports need only exist for the current session.
- Web research, RAG and multiple agents: add dependencies and failure modes beyond the proof of concept.
- Business plans, pitch generation and financial forecasts: outside the validation-focused purpose.
- Viability scores and guaranteed predictions: unsupported precision.

## Open Decisions

- Establish access to volunteer online teachers before conducting the separate manual pilot.
- Verify the exact Gemini model and account limits during technical planning.

The learner approved the core scope with the above domain expansion on September 29, 2026. The product plan is approved; volunteer access remains an operational prerequisite for the pilot.
