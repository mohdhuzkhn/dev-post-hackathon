# Target market and competitor research

Implemented flow: enter an idea and customer geography; receive 2–3 proposed segments; edit each segment's user, needs/buying behavior and buyer; confirm the market; receive the four-domain analysis; request the fifth domain, Local Competitors.

Customer geography is entered manually. The app does not request GPS or infer location. Online / Global is the default. Editing the idea, geography or segments clears earlier analysis and research to prevent stale results. AI segment suggestions remain hypotheses.

Competitor research now uses Tavily basic search. Gemini prepares at most two short queries from the idea, geography and confirmed segments. Tavily returns page URLs and snippets; Gemini extracts candidates using numbered source references. Only valid HTTPS URLs returned by Tavily are rendered. Duplicate pages are merged and unsupported references discarded. Sources do not guarantee every claim or exhaust the market. No matches, missing configuration and provider failures are distinguished; analysis remains available during research errors.

The user approved Tavily as an additional provider and saved TAVILY_API_KEY in the ignored .env.local. The form discloses sharing search queries derived from the idea and confirmed market with Tavily. Credentials stay server-side. At most two basic searches run per click, using one credit each. No automatic retries, paid upgrades, raw-content extraction or generated Tavily answers are requested. Production also requires TAVILY_API_KEY in Netlify.

Verification: live market suggestions returned three segments. Tavily-backed research for the AI teacher-clone idea returned six source-linked candidates in 13 seconds. Automated checks cover input bounds, segment counts, source integrity, search limits, missing keys, malformed responses and sanitized quota/configuration errors.
